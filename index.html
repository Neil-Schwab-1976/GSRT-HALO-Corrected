The pathway

One person, seven steps

A person caught in the cycle of emergency rooms, police contact and shelters is offered an honest choice, not an assignment. Path B leads through a peer-run, land-based camp, with medical care alongside it, never in place of it. It ends in paid work.

  1. Worker conversationA trauma- and addiction-informed first contact
  2. RWOB intakeSafety gate, RAFT, validated screens, colour routing
  3. Choice PointPath A and Path B presented honestly; Path B is never assigned
  4. MASH Antifragile3 months, 200 beds, 20 nodes, shadow pairing in the first month
  5. Senior & mentorIn month 3, shadow a newcomer of your own
  6. DEEDSPROOFGraduation badge: Trust · Stabilize · Begin
  7. GSRT EarthworksPaid environmental restoration at $22/hr

Honest gaps

Still open

These must be settled before a costed proposal goes to a funder.

  • Operator. A non-profit, co-op or Nation-run entity to run the camp, with the G.A.P.S. IP licensed to it.
  • Site and medical partner. Crown land near Cobalt / Coleman Township, or an alternative; a mobile team or RAAM clinic.
  • Year 1 Controllers. 40 hired peer workers at about $45K would take about $1.8M of the $2.25M Operations stream.
  • User Area. Supervised consumption needs a Health Canada s. 56.1 exemption.
  • Earthworks capacity. 200 jobs against about 600 graduates a year.
  • Naming and mapping. Block 10 has no name and Block 4 has two; the G.A.P.S. mapping differs between documents.
  • Retreat map. The week-by-week 3-Day Get-A-Way map for the 13-week stay, and the DEEDSPROOF module fixes.
  • Mathematics Manual. Being corrected; it will be added once fixed.
