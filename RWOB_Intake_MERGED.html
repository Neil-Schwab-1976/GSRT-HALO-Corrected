How the HALO-WBBRY colours work

WHITE — Foundation: steadiness, orientation, clear thinking. Navigation, housing first steps, harm reduction, primary care.

BLUE — Health & Depth: connection, trust, grief. Counselling, outpatient, family therapy, concurrent-disorder care.

YELLOW — Reactivity & Response: stress reactions, cravings, urges. Withdrawal management, RAAM clinics, crisis support.

BLACK — Shadow & Transformation: loss of enjoyment, identity, trauma. Residential, intensive and trauma treatment.

RED — Emergency & Crisis: immediate safety. 24/7 lines, mobile crisis, emergency stabilization.

What this is, plainly: HALO-WBBRY is HALO's own sorting key, inspired by the NFPA 704 hazard-diamond colours. It is not a diagnosis, not a measure of brain chemistry, and not part of or endorsed by any government safety system, including WHMIS.
