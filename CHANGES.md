# What changed from the earlier repository

The earlier public repository (`GSRT---CCGBI---GTT---EDU-REV---AI-ALIGNMENT`) contained the claims and figures below. They are withdrawn or corrected here.

| Earlier claim or figure | Now | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "H-6/6 Historically Unprecedented" | Removed | Self-assigned rating with no external basis |
| "Verified by three AI systems" (and about 100 uses of "verified") | Removed | AI review is not verification. Figures are now marked as modelled, with sources. |
| "$40,000 retires debt in 120 days" | Removed | No derivation or source |
| Kenya pilot, "500×+" return | Removed | No pilot took place, so there is no data |
| WHMIS colour categories (about 130 uses) | HALO-WBBRY colour key, with the inspiration credited to NFPA 704 | WHMIS 2015 has no colour categories and covers only hazardous products. The red/blue/yellow/white scheme is the NFPA 704 diamond. HALO-WBBRY is HALO's own routing key and is not endorsed by any government system. |
| $7,500 per 4-month stay | $7,500 per **3-month** stay | $80 × 90 + $60 × 3 = $7,380. Locked by the author, Oct 18 2026. |
| $3.5M pilot ask | $11.25M Year 1 (about $14.48M with returns) | The old figure covered about one cycle of food and clothing |
| 18.7:1 and 11.9:1 ROI | Break-even framing (23.4% at $80K) | The ratios assumed 100% of costs avoided for all 600 people |
| $120K presented as the sourced cost | Three tracks: $120K Observed, $80K Conservative, $56K Evidence Floor | The agency figures behind $120K could not be traced |
| 10-year LRP value $918,000 | $765,000 | $76,500 × 10 = $765,000. $918,000 needs an unstated ripple factor of 2. |
| E_ab exposure figure inconsistent across documents | $456,500 (A_r = 4) | Reconciled in the workbook and the Addendum |
| "Naming reduces amygdala activation by up to 50% (Siegel, 2012)" | Lieberman et al. (2007), with no percentage | Wrong source, and the percentage doesn't appear in the study |
| "No police. No court." | Clear incident line: the person harmed decides whether police are called | No funder, insurer or medical partner would accept the earlier wording |
| Two separate RWOB intakes | One merged intake | Safety items aligned to C-SSRS wording; US crisis line replaced with NEDIC; unbuilt Hue/Shade/Tint scoring removed; the "worker will call within 48 hours" promise removed |
| Duplicate files, a 1-byte "system flow diagram", a plain-text file named .docx, and links to missing files | Not carried over | Housekeeping |
